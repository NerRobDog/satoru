# Support

Everything here is free and open source, built and tested on my own Macs. If it saved you a
licence or an evening, you can send something back.

What this supports is the engineering: the graphics layer, the Wine builds, the translation
helpers and the work of adapting each game to run on Apple Silicon. No game, and no part of
any game, is sold or distributed for money here. Games you already own stay games you
already own; where a game is published by its rights holder under a non-commercial licence,
we build from those sources and redistribute no content of theirs.
